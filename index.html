A healthcare system that works for every Minnesotan.

A different way of thinking about healthcare

Throughout history, progress has often begun by reconsidering how we understand a problem, not simply working harder within the same assumptions. Changing our thinking does not mean everything that came before was wrong.

Progress does not require us to abandon what works. It requires us to reconsider how the pieces fit together.

Healthcare Minnesota explores what becomes possible when we organize the knowledge, skilled people, effective treatments, and resources already available around dependable care for everyone.

01 / The opportunity

We can begin building a better system today.

Universal care is an achievable objective, not an effortless one. How we organize, deliver, and pay for it remains a practical design challenge. We can start with existing strengths, test workable approaches, measure their results, and improve what falls short.

01 / CARE

Start with the patient

Begin with dependable primary care, continuity, early intervention, and coordination near where people live and work.

02 / RESOURCES

Make funding work

Align existing resources with effective care, and test how to sustain and expand that capacity.

03 / PARTICIPATION

Build it together

Bring communities, clinicians, employers, and institutions into the practical work of building and testing care.

Universal care is the destination. The path begins with building something that works.
